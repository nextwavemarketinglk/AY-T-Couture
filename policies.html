Orders & availability

Product availability, lead times and pricing are confirmed at the point of order. The full build can distinguish ready stock, pre-order, made-to-order and quotation-only products.

Custom & bridal orders

Bespoke and bridal pieces require consultation, measurements and agreed production milestones. Final terms should be confirmed in writing before production begins.

Shipping & collection

Local collection, courier and international delivery options can be configured based on destination, product type and production lead time.

Returns & alterations

Ready-to-wear and custom products may follow different return or alteration policies. Final language should reflect AYĀT’s approved commercial policy.

Privacy

Appointment, order and contact information should be used only for servicing the client relationship and operated under the final privacy policy.
