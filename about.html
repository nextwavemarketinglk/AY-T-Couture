The house

Sri Lankan couture, with a global eye.

AYĀT is positioned as a contemporary couture house where craftsmanship, individuality and the experience around the garment matter equally.

Our story

Built around women, occasion and individuality.

Founded by Dr. Anuradha Wickramasinghe, AYĀT brings together couture, bridal and modern ethnic fashion within a personal client experience. The new digital direction is intentionally restrained: less template, more house identity.

This concept keeps the brand story editorial while making the commercial journey practical — a client can discover a collection, understand whether it is available, pre-order or bespoke, and immediately take the correct next step.
